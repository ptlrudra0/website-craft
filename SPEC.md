# Website Craft: first-release spec

## Purpose
A portable website-creation skill that helps a coding agent choose a coherent direction, use suitable assets and build a convincing site from a sparse business brief. It should produce intentional variations, not the same page with different colours.

## Start every new website request
Invite a reference: "For a more tailored result, share a reference from Behance, Cosmos or Dribbble. It helps clarify layout, typography and mood. No reference? We can work from your brief."
Do not block on a reference. Ask only for missing business/audience/primary-action facts that materially change the site. Gather essential questions together, offer defaults and summarize the plan before implementation. Do not invent testimonials, metrics, certifications, prices or product screenshots.

## Public deliverables
- Original portable skill and chat copy/paste guide, documented native-agent setup and honest compatibility matrix.
- Industry catalog with alternative directions and asset-selection manifests, distinguishing bundled files, source-linked candidates and planned coverage.
- Initial demonstration pair in technology business and farm/poultry, with same-brief variants to expose template repetition. Additional categories can be documented as direction recipes, not claimed tested examples.
- Optional credited Scroll-craft reference, not automatic installation or paid generation.
- Source/rights notices and documentation of actual visual/responsive/accessibility checks and limits.

## Variety model
Do not choose a style from the industry name alone. Consider audience, business job, content evidence, visual material and desired mood. Present multiple coherent plans that differ in information order, composition, typography, image treatment and interaction. Preserve explicit user direction; variation is not an excuse to ignore a reference or choose random novelty. Asset count alone is not a quality claim.

## Assets
Choose by purpose: a software workflow diagram is not a substitute for actual product evidence; a farm photo must not falsely depict a real business or certify welfare/organic status. Use coherent colour treatment with readable text, but do not wash meaningful product colours beyond recognition. Bundle original or explicitly redistributable assets with their notices. Link to design inspiration and stock-source candidates rather than mirror uncertain collections. Record source, licence, delivery mode, role, tone/crop guidance, alt guidance and fallback per item. Behance/Cosmos/Dribbble inclusion does not imply reuse rights.

## First-release acceptance
- Two clear industry examples; at least one same-brief pair with material differences beyond palette.
- Real functional controls or plainly labelled demos. No fake form submission, client testimonials or unsupported claims.
- Inspect desktop and phone pixels, keyboard focus, text overflow, reduced motion and selected contrast pairs. Record what was actually tested; no blanket certification.
- Portable text can be read by a chatbot; file editing/browser/deployment capabilities depend on the host. No untested universal-runtime claim.
- Public-files review excludes private context/account information, unrelated projects and private notes. MIT applies to original work; third-party assets retain their own terms.

## Status
Early preview scope documented. Initial examples locally checked; native-agent tests and broader asset collection remain open. Competitors were compared through public documentation, not controlled end-to-end runs. No claim of beating all existing skills or earning a star count.
