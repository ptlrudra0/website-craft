Farm produce, understood through the range, pack information and a working enquiry list. Warm burgundy, cream and selected farm/food photography.

Ten pages. Prepare and download an enquiry or regular-supply brief. Nothing is sent and no order is placed. Stock photographs are illustrative, not this fictional farm's facilities or inventory. Photography retains separate source terms.

An original browser rhythm instrument, with actual pattern editing, synthesis and WAV generation. Cobalt, warm yellow and a large typographic rhythm.
