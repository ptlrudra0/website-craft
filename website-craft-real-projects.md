These are existing public projects, linked rather than copied. Inspect the actual page and the job it does before adapting a technique. They are not generic templates, cleared asset packs or proof of production readiness. Original sites stay untouched.

https://ptlrudra0.github.io/black-archive-navigation/

Request no geolocation/sensor permissions simply to inspect the visual example.

## What transfers across them

1. One idea governs type, spacing, image treatment and interaction across the full site.
2. The graphic explains the subject: clouds/contours for an exploratory portfolio, branded scenes for a creative business, signal continuity for navigation.
3. Content chooses the structure; do not force all briefs into a repeated hero/card/testimonial template.
4. Working interactions serve the subject, not arbitrary feature counts. Preserve clear reading states and reduced-motion paths.
5. Existing-site quality is a reference, not a certificate. Verify actual responsive pixels, links, controls, error/fallback states, asset rights and launch constraints in the new build.
