# Website Craft: current edition

The current full skill and its relative references/assets are in [the revised download](../../1-website-craft-real-projects-source.zip). Extract it and use the complete skills/website-craft folder rather than mixing this repository's older supporting files with the revised skill.

[Read the current skill](../../website-craft-v2-SKILL.md) and [existing-project notes](../../website-craft-real-projects.md).

The primary examples are [Sky Grid final portfolio](https://ptlrudra0.github.io/portfolio-previews-50/final.html), [Aquilix preview14](https://ptlrudra0.github.io/aquilix/preview14/) and [Black Archive navigation](https://ptlrudra0.github.io/black-archive-navigation/). They replace the invented product studies as the example set. Original websites stay untouched and their content/media/identity are not licensed stock assets.

Study the complete relevant journey, coherent art direction, subject-specific graphics, purposeful interaction and readable motion-rest states. Derive a new design contract from the real brief, rather than copying one example's palette/layout into every project. Existing websites are references, not proof of universal production readiness, accessibility, performance or device support. Verify the actual new build.
