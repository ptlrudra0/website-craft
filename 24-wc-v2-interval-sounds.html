THE MATERIAL / ORIGINAL SYNTHESIS

THREE
SMALL SOUNDS.

No samples. No borrowed records.
Just simple shapes moving air.

01

THUMP.

A sine wave falls quickly from 120 to 45 Hz, then fades. This is the low anchor, with a quarter-second tail.

02

CRACK.

Seeded noise and a 180 Hz tone decay together. A dry accent, about 0.15 seconds long. Not a recording of a snare drum.

03

TICK.

A quick burst of high-passed seeded noise. Just enough texture to mark the smaller intervals. Less than 0.08 seconds.

THE PATTERN
IS THE POINT.

Small ingredients leave the arrangement exposed. One missing step can change the whole bar.

Waveform plots above come from each generated sound at unit gain, not measurements of your speakers or the current loop. The instrument synthesizes the same sound model for live playback and downloaded WAVs.
